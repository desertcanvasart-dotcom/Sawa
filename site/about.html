سوا · together

Nobody should lose
a trip to bad timing.

Sawa is the Arabic word for "together." We built it to fix one quietly painful problem in Egyptian travel — and to make sure that when someone wants to see the pyramids, the Nile or the desert, the trip they were promised actually happens.

The problem we saw

Everyone was losing.

People come to Egypt for all kinds of reasons — it's affordable, it's extraordinary, and for many it feels safer to explore in a group. They'd arrive ready to join a group tour, only to discover the group didn't exist. The traveler left disappointed.

Meanwhile operators were getting those same requests — one traveler here, two there — never quite enough to run the date. So they cancelled. The tour they wanted to run, and the trip the traveler wanted to take, both vanished over a gap of two or three people.

Demand existed. Supply existed. They just never met on the same date. That's the gap Sawa closes.

The idea

Make everybody win.

One shared departure, built from everyone's travelers. The minute four people commit to the same date, it becomes real for all of them.

Travelers win

A real group, a confirmed trip, and no solo surcharge — without paying a cent until it's locked in.

Operators win

The dates they'd have cancelled now run, filled by demand pooled across the whole network.

Egypt wins

More trips actually happen — more livelihoods for local guides, more travelers seeing the real country.

The mark, explained

Every part of the Sawa mark is a promise.

Four travelers

The four dots are the minimum to confirm a departure. Below four it's a plan — at four it's a trip.

One shared route

The S-path is the single journey everyone's on. Different operators, different travelers — one road.

The GoAhead

The gold dot is the moment of truth. When it lights up, your tour is confirmed, paid and really running.

What we stand for

Trust is the product.

We're a marketplace, but what we really sell is certainty — for the traveler and the operator alike. Every decision we make protects that.

No group, no charge

Travelers never pay for a trip that isn't confirmed.

Only verified operators

Licence, insurance and track record, checked before listing.

Local first

Egyptian operators and guides keep their brand, their margin and their livelihood.
