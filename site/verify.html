Operator application

Get the gold shield.

Verification is what travelers trust and what lets you list departures. It's a one-time check of the things that keep travelers safe — licence, insurance and track record. Most operators are reviewed within a few business days.

What you'll need

Four things to get verified

A valid Egyptian tourism licence

Issued by the Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities. We confirm it's active.

Passenger & liability insurance

Proof of current cover for the tours you intend to run.

Track record

Two references or a portfolio of past tours — and links to any existing reviews.

A payout account

Bank or accepted payout method for receiving funds after each GoAhead.
