The symbol, explained

Every part of the Sawa mark is a promise.

It isn't decoration. The circle, the route and the gold dot each stand for how a shared departure actually works.

Four travelers

The four dots are the minimum to confirm a departure. Below four it stays a plan — at four it becomes a trip.

One shared route

The S-path is the single journey everyone is on. Different operators, different travelers — one road, one guide, one group.

GoAhead

The single gold dot is the moment of truth. When it lights up, your tour is confirmed, paid and really running.
