The process

Three steps from a maybe to a confirmed trip.

Watch the same departure fill up on the right as you read each step.

01
The spark

A date gets started — by an operator, or by you

A licensed Egyptian operator lists a date they'd love to run — or a traveler starts one right on any tour page: pick the tour, pick your day, and it becomes a departure others can join. No upfront cost, no risk: it only departs if the group forms.

02
The travelers

Travelers join from everywhere

You and a friend, a couple from Berlin, an agency's three — all land on one shared departure. Everyone sees the seats fill in real time, and the price stays fixed no matter how the group is sourced.

03
The GoAhead

Four travelers, and the gold dot turns on

The minute the fourth traveler joins, the date locks and everyone gets the GoAhead. Payments are released to the operator, the trip is certain, and you travel together. If it never reaches four, nobody is charged. Once confirmed, it runs — we don't cancel a date for low numbers, so if someone drops out afterwards your trip is unaffected.

Why it's safe

The rules that protect everyone on board.

You only pay if it runs

Your payment is held securely and only released to the operator once the departure reaches GoAhead. No confirmation, no charge.

Every operator is Ministry-licensed

Every operator on Sawa is registered with the Egyptian Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities and registered with ETAA before a single departure goes live — never freelancers or unregistered guides, as open marketplaces often allow. You always know who you're traveling with.

Small groups, fixed price

Departures are capped at a small size — usually 4 to 8 — and your price never changes based on how the group fills up.

Real dates, no bait

Every date on the board is real — listed by a Ministry-licensed operator, or started by a traveler and reviewed by our team before it opens. If a group doesn't form, you're refunded in full or moved to the next date — your choice.

Questions

Good to know

What exactly is a "shared departure"?

One tour, on one date, filled by travelers pooled from several Ministry-licensed operators instead of just one. Same boat, same guide, same price for everyone.

Can I start my own date?

Yes. Every tour page has a "start your own date" option — pick the day that suits you and it becomes a departure others can join. Our team reviews each new date before it appears, and nothing is charged unless it reaches GoAhead.

When is a trip confirmed?

The moment four travelers join the same date. That's the GoAhead — the date locks and the trip is certain to run.

What if a date doesn't reach four?

You're never charged for a trip that doesn't run. You get a full refund or a free move to the next departure — whichever you prefer.

Who will I travel with?

A small mix of travelers — typically 4 to 8 — with one licensed guide for the whole group throughout the trip.

How do payments work?

You reserve now and pay securely through Sawa. Funds are only released to the operator once the departure is confirmed.

Can operators set their own price?

Yes. Operators publish the itinerary and price; Sawa keeps it fixed for every traveler regardless of how the seats are sourced.
