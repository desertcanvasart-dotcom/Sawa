For travelers

Booking & the GoAhead

The essentials on reserving, paying and travelling with Sawa.

How you're protected

It's the moment a departure becomes real. Every Sawa trip needs a minimum number of travelers — usually four — committed to the same date. When that number is reached, the date locks, everyone is charged, and the tour is confirmed to run. We mark it with a gold dot.

Only at GoAhead. Reserving a seat is free; we may place a temporary authorisation hold to verify your card, but no money moves until the group confirms. Until then you can cancel with one click.

The departure auto-cancels at its deadline and you pay nothing. Any hold is released in full by your bank, and we'll suggest similar dates that are closer to confirming.

Yes, subject to the operator's cancellation policy, which is shown on every departure before you reserve. Most offer a full refund up to a set number of days before departure.

A licensed Egyptian operator that Sawa has verified — you'll see their name, licence status and rating on every departure. Sawa coordinates the group and your payment; the operator delivers the experience.

A Sawa departure only runs as a real group with a local guide, so you're never setting out alone. Every operator is licensed and insured, you get the full itinerary up front, and a 24/7 support line throughout.

Absolutely. You can reserve multiple seats in one booking — and your travelers count toward the group's GoAhead, so bringing someone helps confirm the date faster.

It varies by tour, but each departure lists exactly what's included and excluded before you book — guiding, entries, accommodation and meals where applicable. Flights and visas are usually separate.
