Three steps to live

Installed before your coffee's cold.

01

Get your operator ID

When your account is verified, you'll find a unique operator-id in your dashboard under Widget.

02

Paste the snippet

Drop the code on any page where you want departures to appear — a dedicated "Group tours" page works best.

03

Style & publish

Set your accent color and default region in the dashboard. Save, publish, and you're collecting travelers.

<!-- 1. Load Sawa once, before </body> --> <script src="https://embed.sawatours.com/v1.js" async></script> <!-- 2. Drop this where departures should show --> <div class="sawa-embed" data-sawa-operator="your-id" data-region="all" data-accent="#17323A" data-limit="6"></div>

Options

data-regionall · cairo · nile · desert · redsea
data-accentany hex color for buttons & bars
data-limithow many departures to show
data-layoutlist · grid · compact
