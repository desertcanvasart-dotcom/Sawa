Put live departures
on your own site.

The Sawa widget shows forming and confirmed departures right on your website, styled to match your brand. Visitors join a group without leaving your page — and every booking helps fill the dates you listed. No developer required.

Get your snippet See it live

One-paste install

Copy a single snippet into your site — Wix, WordPress, Squarespace or hand-coded. It renders instantly, no build step.

Blends into your site

It picks up your page's background, text and link colors automatically, and resizes itself to fit — no settings to manage.

Credited to you

Every traveler who books through your widget is tracked to your agency's referral code, so the traffic you already have is counted as yours.

Three steps to live

Installed before your coffee's cold.

01

Open Promote

Once your operator account is approved, the Promote section of your dashboard shows your referral code and ready-made snippets with it filled in.

02

Paste the snippet

Drop it on any page where you want Sawa to appear — a dedicated "Group tours" page works best.

03

Publish

It takes your page's background, text and link colors by itself and sizes itself to fit. Every booking it sends is credited to your agency.

<!-- Sawa widget: paste where it should appear --> <iframe src="https://sawa.tours/embed/book?ref=YOUR-CODE" style="width:100%;border:0;border-radius:18px" loading="lazy" data-sawa-embed></iframe> <!-- Sizes it to fit and matches your page's colors --> <script> (function(){var q=new URLSearchParams(location.search),b=q.get('sawa-booking'),k=q.get('sawa-confirm'),j=q.get('sawa-join'); function s(f){try{var c=getComputedStyle(document.body),a=document.querySelector('a');f.contentWindow.postMessage({type:'sawa-embed-theme',bg:c.backgroundColor,text:c.color,accent:a?getComputedStyle(a).color:'',font:c.fontFamily,page:location.href},'*');}catch(e){}} if(b||k||j){var w=document.querySelector('iframe[data-sawa-embed][src*="/embed/book"]');if(w){try{var u=new URL(w.src);u.pathname=b?'/embed/booking/'+encodeURIComponent(b):k?'/embed/confirm/'+encodeURIComponent(k):'/embed/join/'+encodeURIComponent(j);w.loading='eager';w.src=u.toString();w.scrollIntoView({block:'start'});}catch(e){}}} addEventListener('message',function(e){if(!e.data)return;document.querySelectorAll('iframe[data-sawa-embed]').forEach(function(f){if(f.contentWindow!==e.source)return;if(e.data.type==='sawa-embed-height')f.style.height=e.data.height+'px';if(e.data.type==='sawa-embed-ready')s(f);if(e.data.type==='sawa-embed-top'&&f.getBoundingClientRect().top<0)f.scrollIntoView({behavior:'smooth',block:'start'});});}); addEventListener('load',function(){document.querySelectorAll('iframe[data-sawa-embed]').forEach(s);});})(); </script>

What it can show

/embed/book?ref=YOUR-CODEevery tour, or the ones you choose: read, pick a date and book on your page
/embed/book/tour/TOUR-ID?ref=YOUR-CODEone day tour, booked on your page
/embed/book/package/PACKAGE-ID?ref=YOUR-CODEone package, booked on your page

Promote builds each of these for you — pick a tour or package there and copy its snippet.
