Three steps to live

Installed before your coffee's cold.

01

Open Promote

Once your operator account is approved, the Promote section of your dashboard shows your referral code and ready-made snippets with it filled in.

02

Paste the snippet

Drop it on any page where you want Sawa to appear — a dedicated "Group tours" page works best.

03

Publish

It takes your page's background, text and link colours by itself and sizes itself to fit. Every booking it sends is credited to your agency.

<!-- Sawa widget: paste where it should appear --> <iframe src="https://sawa.tours/embed?ref=YOUR-CODE" style="width:100%;border:0;border-radius:18px" loading="lazy" data-sawa-embed></iframe> <!-- Sizes it to fit and matches your page's colours --> <script> (function(){function s(f){try{var c=getComputedStyle(document.body),a=document.querySelector('a');f.contentWindow.postMessage({type:'sawa-embed-theme',bg:c.backgroundColor,text:c.color,accent:a?getComputedStyle(a).color:''},'*');}catch(e){}} addEventListener('message',function(e){if(!e.data)return;document.querySelectorAll('iframe[data-sawa-embed]').forEach(function(f){if(f.contentWindow!==e.source)return;if(e.data.type==='sawa-embed-height')f.style.height=e.data.height+'px';if(e.data.type==='sawa-embed-ready')s(f);if(e.data.type==='sawa-embed-top'&&f.getBoundingClientRect().top<0)f.scrollIntoView({behavior:'smooth',block:'start'});});}); addEventListener('load',function(){document.querySelectorAll('iframe[data-sawa-embed]').forEach(s);});})(); </script>

What it can show

/embed?ref=YOUR-CODEa Sawa banner leading to the whole catalogue
/embed/tour/TOUR-ID?ref=YOUR-CODEone day tour, with its live shared price
/embed/package/PACKAGE-ID?ref=YOUR-CODEone package, with its live shared price

Promote builds each of these for you — pick a tour or package there and copy its snippet.
