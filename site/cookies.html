Cookie Policy.

Which cookies sawa.tours sets, what each one is for, and how to control or delete them.

Effective date: August 8, 2026 · Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Capital Travel Service, trading as Sawa Tours (“Sawa”, “we”, “us” or “our”), uses cookies and similar technologies on sawa.tours, its related booking pages and dashboards, and the Sawa embedded departures widget. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a browser or device. They can remember a session, a preference or an identifier. Websites also use related technologies such as local storage and session storage, which keep data in the browser in the same way but are not sent with every request. This Policy calls all of them “cookies” for simplicity, and the table below says which kind each one actually is.

First-party cookies are set by Sawa. Third-party cookies are set by another provider whose service appears on the Platform. Session cookies expire when the browser closes; persistent cookies remain until their stated expiry or until you delete them.

2. Cookie categories

Strictly necessary

Required for security, booking, login, fraud prevention or remembering your choices. The requested service cannot work reliably without them.

Functional

Remember optional choices, such as the partner link you arrived through, and provide enhanced features.

Analytics

Help us understand visits, traffic sources, errors and feature use, so that we can improve the Platform.

Advertising

Sawa does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data derived from cookies to anyone. If that ever changes, this Policy and the table below will be updated before the change takes effect.

4. Analytics and your choices

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to understand how people find and use the site. We have not enabled Google’s advertising features, Google Signals or User-ID, and we do not use analytics data to identify you personally.

Sawa does not currently show a cookie banner, and Google Analytics loads on every page of the site. We are being straightforward about that rather than describing a choice we do not yet offer. Until a consent banner is in place, you can stop analytics entirely by:

  • installing Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on, which applies on every site you visit;
  • blocking third-party cookies, or blocking googletagmanager.com, in your browser settings; or
  • using your browser’s tracking-protection or private-browsing mode.

Google’s own handling of this data is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Blocking analytics does not affect browsing or booking. Blocking the strictly necessary sign-in storage only affects the operator and staff dashboards; the public site works without any sign-in.

5. Referral attribution

When you follow a link from a partner, Sawa stores a short referral code in your browser for 30 days so that the correct partner is credited if you reserve or book. The code does not contain your name or contact details, though it can become linked to a booking once you submit one. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for sawa.tours.

6. The embedded Sawa widget

Sawa departures may appear inside a partner operator’s website. Loading the widget sends Sawa ordinary network information, such as your IP address, browser details, the referring domain and the widget configuration. The referral store described above is not set by the widget itself — only by a real visit to sawa.tours.

The partner website controls its own cookies and should identify Sawa in its own privacy and cookie notices. Once you submit information to Sawa through the widget, our Privacy Policy applies to Sawa’s handling of it.

7. Third-party content

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and tour photography from our storage provider. Serving those files involves your IP address, as any web request does, but neither sets cookies for us. Where a page later includes payment forms, video, maps, chat or similar embedded services, those providers may set their own cookies, and they will be added to the table above.

8. Browser controls

Most browsers let you inspect, block or delete cookies and site data. Blocking every cookie can affect secure sign-in, booking and saved preferences on any website. Instructions are available from:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

9. Changes to this Policy

We update this Policy and the table above whenever a technology, purpose, provider or retention period changes. If we add a cookie or change what one is used for, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about cookies or your data are welcome at hello@sawa.tours or on WhatsApp.
