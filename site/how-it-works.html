The process

Three steps from a maybe to a confirmed trip.

01
The operator

A verified operator posts a route

A licensed Egyptian operator lists a date and itinerary they'd love to run but can't fill on their own — say, a Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan. No upfront cost, no risk: it only departs if the group forms.

02
The travelers

Travelers join from everywhere

Your two travelers, our four, another agency's three — all land on one shared departure. Everyone sees the seats fill in real time, and the price stays fixed no matter how the group is sourced.

03
The GoAhead

Four travelers, and the gold dot turns on

The minute the fourth traveler joins, the date locks and everyone gets the GoAhead. Payments are released to the operator, the trip is certain, and you travel together. If it never reaches four, nobody is charged.

Why it's safe

The rules that protect everyone on board.

You only pay if it runs

Your payment is held securely and only released to the operator once the departure reaches GoAhead. No confirmation, no charge.

Every operator is verified

Each Egyptian operator is licensed, vetted and rated by Sawa before a single departure goes live. You always know who you're travelling with.

Small groups, fixed price

Departures are capped at a small size — usually 4 to 8 — and your price never changes based on how the group fills up.

Real dates, no bait

Every listing is a date an operator genuinely intends to run. If a group doesn't form, you're refunded in full or moved to the next date — your choice.

Questions

Good to know

What exactly is a "shared departure"?

One tour, on one date, filled by travelers pooled from several verified operators instead of just one. Same boat, same guide, same price for everyone.

When is a trip confirmed?

The moment four travelers join the same date. That's the GoAhead — the date locks and the trip is certain to run.

What if a date doesn't reach four?

You're never charged for a trip that doesn't run. You get a full refund or a free move to the next departure — whichever you prefer.

Who will I travel with?

A small mix of travelers — typically 4 to 8 — with one licensed guide for the whole group throughout the trip.

How do payments work?

You reserve now and pay securely through Sawa. Funds are only released to the operator once the departure is confirmed.

Can operators set their own price?

Yes. Operators publish the itinerary and price; Sawa keeps it fixed for every traveler regardless of how the seats are sourced.
