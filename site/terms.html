3. Key definitions

8. GoAhead and formation of the Booking

A Departure reaches GoAhead when the number of valid traveler commitments reaches the Confirmation Minimum shown for that Departure. We then send the Lead Traveler a GoAhead confirmation stating:

  • the Departure and Operating Partner;
  • the travelers included;
  • the Tour Price and currency;
  • the deposit or full amount due;
  • the payment deadline and balance-due date;
  • the applicable cancellation conditions; and
  • important itinerary-specific requirements.

Your Booking becomes binding when we have sent the GoAhead confirmation and you have paid the amount it requires by the deadline it states. The contract for the tour forms when that payment is received. We do not charge a card automatically at GoAhead.

If payment fails or is not received by the stated deadline, we may release the seats after giving the Lead Traveler a reasonable opportunity to correct the payment, unless departure is imminent.

Once a Departure reaches GoAhead it will not be canceled merely because another traveler later cancels and the group falls below the Confirmation Minimum.

9. Confirmation Deadlines

Unless a different deadline is clearly shown before you reserve:

  • a day tour must reach GoAhead at least 7 days before departure.

If a Forming Departure does not reach the Confirmation Minimum by its Confirmation Deadline, it closes automatically. You owe nothing and no Tour Price is captured. We notify you by email as soon as this happens, and we may suggest another Departure, but you are not required to accept it.
