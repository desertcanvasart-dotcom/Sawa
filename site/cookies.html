Cookie Policy.

Which cookies sawa.tours sets, what each one is for, and how to control or delete them.

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Capital Travel Service, trading as Sawa Tours (“Sawa Tours”, “us”, “we”, or “our”) operates the sawa.tours website (the “Service”). This page explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and the choices you have. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They let the site remember things between pages and visits — that you are signed in, or how you found us — without storing anything on our servers about your device itself. Similar technologies such as local storage work the same way, and this policy covers them too.

Cookies we use

CookieTypePurpose
Session cookieStrictly necessaryKeeps operators and staff signed in to their dashboard. Set only when you sign in; without it the portal cannot work. Not used for tracking.
ReferralFunctionalWhen you arrive through a partner link, remembers the referral so the partner is credited if you book. Contains a short code, nothing personal.
Google Analytics (_ga and related)AnalyticsHelps us understand how the site is used — which pages are visited and how travellers find us — so we can improve it. Statistics are aggregated; we do not use them to identify you.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data derived from cookies to anyone.

Third parties

Google Analytics is provided by Google LLC; its cookies are governed by Google’s privacy policy, and you can opt out of Analytics everywhere with the browser opt-out add-on. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some tour photography from our storage provider; serving those files involves your IP address as part of any web request, but neither sets cookies for us.

Managing cookies

You can see, block and delete cookies in your browser settings. Every major browser lets you refuse all cookies, refuse third-party cookies only, or clear them when you close the window:

  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge

Blocking analytics cookies does not affect browsing or booking. Blocking the strictly necessary session cookie only affects signing in to the operator and staff dashboards — the public site works without any sign-in.

Changes to this policy

If we add a cookie or change what one is used for, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about cookies or your data are welcome at hello@sawa.tours or on WhatsApp.
