No group, no charge

Your trip only
costs you when it's real.

Sawa was built to end one specific disappointment: paying for a group tour that never actually runs. So we made the rule simple. You reserve your seat for free. Payment only becomes due when four travelers confirm the same date and the GoAhead lights up — a deposit then, the balance before you travel. If the date never confirms, you pay nothing. No card is charged and no hold is placed. All prices are in euros (EUR).

Confirmed means confirmed. Four travelers on the same date is what confirms it. From that moment we don't cancel it for low numbers — if someone drops out afterwards, your trip still runs.

Never charged if it doesn't run

Every date has a deadline to reach four travelers — 30 days before departure on a multi-day package, 7 days on a day tour, and always shown on the departure before you reserve. Miss it and the date is canceled automatically, you're emailed, and nothing is charged — there was never anything to refund. No fee, no fine print.

What happens to your money

From reserve to refund, step by step.

Total transparency on every stage of a booking — because the whole point of Sawa is that you never wonder whether your trip is real.

01

You reserve a seat — €0 EUR taken

You pick an open departure and reserve. Reserving a seat costs nothing. No card is charged and no hold is placed, and no money leaves your account. You can cancel anytime while the group is still forming.

02

The group fills in real time

You watch the seat counter climb as travelers from across Sawa's operators join the same date. You'll get an email the moment it's one seat away.

03

GoAhead — the date locks, payment begins

When the fourth traveler joins, the departure is confirmed and the operator is notified to run the tour. Only now does payment become due: a deposit on the per-person price you saw — 10% on a day tour and 25% on a multi-day package — with the balance due before departure. If the date confirms within 30 days of departure, the full amount is due at confirmation. Your exact figures and balance date are shown before you reserve and on your confirmation.

04

It never fills? Auto-cancel, nothing charged

If the deadline passes without four travelers, the date is canceled for everyone. No card is charged and no hold is placed, so there is nothing to release. You're free to join another departure.

Every operator is Ministry-licensed

You travel with licensed Egyptian operators — never a stranger.

Sawa pools travelers, but the tour is always run by a real local operator licensed by the Ministry of Tourism. Before any company can list a departure, we check the things that actually keep you safe — Sawa never lists freelancers or unregistered entities, as open marketplaces often do.

We confirm registration with the Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities before a single seat is listed.

Insurance & liability cover

Operators carry the passenger and liability insurance required to run guided tours.

A track record we can check
