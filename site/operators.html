The math that never works

Two travelers isn't a tour.
Pooled, it is.

A guide, a vehicle and a permit cost the same whether two people show up or eight. Below your minimum, every date is a loss — so it gets canceled. Sawa changes the input: your two travelers join four of ours and three from another agency, and suddenly the date clears its minimum and runs.

How operators use Sawa

Live a route in minutes.

No integration, no upfront fee, no risk to your reputation.

01

Apply to list

Submit your Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities license and insurance once. Most operators are reviewed within a few business days.

02

List a date you'd love to run

Set the itinerary, minimum group size, price and deadline. It appears on the open board and on partner sites.

03

The network fills it

Travelers from across Sawa — and from other operators' widgets — join your date. You watch it fill in your dashboard.

04

GoAhead — you get paid & run it

At the minimum, the date locks and travelers are charged. You receive the manifest and payout, and deliver the tour under your own brand.
