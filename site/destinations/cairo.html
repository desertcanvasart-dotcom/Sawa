Why Cairo

The only city where you can stand between the pyramids and a medieval bazaar in one afternoon.

Cairo is loud, layered and unforgettable. Giza's pyramids sit on the western edge; across the river the new Grand Egyptian Museum holds Tutankhamun's complete treasure, and the old city winds through mosques, churches and spice markets that have traded for a thousand years.

Most Sawa travelers use Cairo as their first stop — a two-to-four day base before flying south to the Nile. Because departures here fill quickly, they reach GoAhead faster than almost anywhere in Egypt.

Good to know

Before you go to Cairo.

Best season

October to April is cool and clear. In summer, tours start early to beat the heat on the open plateau.

Getting around

Traffic is heavy — every Sawa tour includes a private guided vehicle, so you’re never fighting for a taxi.

How long to stay

Two days covers the essentials; three or four adds Saqqara, the museum and the old city without rushing.

Pairs well with

Fly south afterwards for a Nile cruise from Luxor — many travelers link the two into one trip.
