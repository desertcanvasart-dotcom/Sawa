Operator application

List your departures on Sawa.

You'll need a current Ministry of Tourism license, ETAA registration, valid insurance, and a track record we can check. Most applications are reviewed within a few business days.

What you'll need

Four things you'll need

A valid Egyptian tourism license

Issued by the Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities. We confirm it's active.

Passenger & liability insurance

Proof of current cover for the tours you intend to run.

Track record

Two references or a portfolio of past tours — and links to any existing reviews.

A payout account

Bank or accepted payout method for receiving funds after each GoAhead.

After you apply

01

Review

We confirm your documents with the Ministry — usually 2–4 business days.

02

Onboarding call

A short call to set up payouts and your first listings.

03

Go live

List your routes and grab your widget snippet.
